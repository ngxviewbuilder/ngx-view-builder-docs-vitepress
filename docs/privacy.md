# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 21 October 2026*

This policy covers **ngxviewbuilder.io**, **demo.ngxviewbuilder.io** and **console.ngxviewbuilder.io** (together, "the Site"), operated by Heyde Labs, MB, company code 308168362, Europos pr. 34-47, LT-46370 Kaunas, Lithuania, the company behind NGX View Builder ("we", "us"). For questions, contact **[info@heydelabs.com](mailto:info@heydelabs.com)**.

## What we collect

**Product page and documentation.** After you accept the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how ngxviewbuilder.io is used, both the product page and the docs: pages visited, approximate location (derived from a truncated/anonymized IP), device and browser type, referrer, on-site search terms, and a small number of custom interaction events (e.g. which code snippets get copied, which demo tab you open, which links people follow from the product page). Your answer to the banner applies to the whole of ngxviewbuilder.io, so you are asked once. If you decline or don't respond to the banner, no analytics data is sent and no analytics cookie is set.

**Live demo.** The structure you build in the demo (`/demo`) is saved only in your browser's local storage. We do not receive, store, or process it on any server.

**Buying a license and the console.** When you buy a license we keep what we need to deliver and support it: your name, e-mail address and company (and VAT number, if you give one), your order and its amount, the licenses and keys issued, the people you add to your organization, and the support conversations you start in the console. We never see your card or bank details: payment happens in Paddle's checkout, and Paddle.com processes it as our Merchant of Record under [its own privacy policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/privacy).

**License checks.** The builder can send its license key and the domain it runs on (`window.location.hostname`) to our license service, and the hosted AI access (MCP) server checks the key the same way. We use this only to confirm that the key is genuine and active.

**Contacting us.** If you email us, we process your email address and message content only to respond to you.

## Cookies and local storage

| Name | Purpose | Set when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `nvb-analytics-consent` | Remembers your cookie-banner choice | Always (strictly necessary, and not a tracking cookie) |
| Google Analytics cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`) | Distinguish visitors and sessions for analytics | Only after you accept |
| Demo structure (`localStorage`) | Auto-saves what you build in the live demo | When you use `/demo` |

## Legal basis

Analytics processing relies on your **consent** (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain, after which the banner reappears on your next visit. Responding to emails relies on our **legitimate interest** in providing support.

## Third parties

**Paddle.com** sells the licenses for us as Merchant of Record and handles payments, invoices, taxes and refunds. E-mails from the console (sign-in links, receipts for license changes, support replies) are sent through **Resend**, with click and open tracking turned off. We use **Google Analytics** (Google Ireland Limited) as a data processor for the analytics described above. Google's own privacy policy and data-transfer safeguards apply to its processing: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Video lessons in the documentation are hosted on **YouTube** (Google Ireland Limited). A page with a lesson shows only a picture stored on this site; the YouTube player loads from youtube-nocookie.com when you press play, and from then on YouTube's privacy policy applies to that video. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

## Retention

Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics account settings. Emails are kept as long as needed to handle your inquiry and any follow-up. Account, order and license records are kept while your account exists and afterwards for as long as Lithuanian accounting and tax law requires.

## Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email **[info@heydelabs.com](mailto:info@heydelabs.com)**. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority: in Lithuania, the [State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI)](https://vdai.lrv.lt/).

## Children

The Site is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The "last updated" date above will reflect any change.
